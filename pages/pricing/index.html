FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Credits are the core unit of compute and processing on the OneAtlas platform. Every time your deployed AI models generate a response, process incoming data, or execute a background workflow, a tiny fraction of credits is consumed. The exact amount of credits used depends heavily on the specific model you choose (e.g., standard models cost fewer credits than advanced reasoning models) and the overall complexity and length of the request. You can monitor your real-time credit usage directly from your project dashboard to ensure you stay within your limits.

We will notify you via email and dashboard alerts when you reach 80% and 100% of your monthly credit allowance. If you fully exhaust your credits, your active workflows and API endpoints will be temporarily paused until the start of your next billing cycle. To prevent any disruption to your production applications, you can either upgrade to a higher tier with a larger monthly allowance, or enable "Auto-Scale" to automatically purchase on-demand credit packs as needed.

Absolutely. Custom domains are fully supported on the Builder plan and above. While the Explorer plan provides a free `.oneatlas.app` subdomain for testing, upgrading allows you to connect your own custom domain for a professional, white-labeled experience. Our Studio and Scale plans go a step further by supporting multiple custom domains per project, complete with automated SSL certificate provisioning and advanced DNS routing capabilities.

No, the credits included in your monthly subscription plan do not roll over to the following month. Your credit balance is automatically reset to your plan's full allowance on your billing date each month. However, if you purchase additional "on-demand" credit packs outside of your regular subscription, those specific credits will remain in your account and do not expire for 12 months.

Yes, OneAtlas is a complete end-to-end platform. Every project you create comes with a fully managed, serverless backend. This includes a dedicated PostgreSQL database for your application data, built-in secure authentication (including SSO options on higher tiers), edge functions for custom backend logic, and scalable blob storage. You don't need to string together multiple different cloud providers; everything you need to build and scale is included out of the box.

Yes, team collaboration is a core feature designed for fast-moving startups and organizations. Starting on the Studio plan, you can invite team members to your shared workspaces. Multiple developers can collaborate on workflows, manage database schemas, and view analytics simultaneously. The Scale plan introduces granular role-based access control (RBAC), allowing you to assign specific permissions (like Admin, Developer, or Viewer) to ensure secure and structured team management.

We maintain an agnostic approach to AI, giving you access to a wide and constantly updated ecosystem of state-of-the-art models. While the Explorer plan grants access to highly capable core models, upgrading to Builder or Studio unlocks advanced reasoning and premium frontier models. Scale users benefit from priority API access and the fastest possible inference speeds.
